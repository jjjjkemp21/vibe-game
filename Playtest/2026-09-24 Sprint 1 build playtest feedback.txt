LURE - PLAYTEST FEEDBACK (Sprint 1 build, ready 2026-09-24 late evening)
=========================================================================
This build: main 4be9e11, pushed to GitHub. It covers Sprint 1 (line + fight fixes) and Sprint 4 (the cooler),
plus the first piece of Sprint 5 (a clock on the HUD) and new fish fight animations.

How to use this file
- Write anything, in any order, as short or long as you like. Bullet points are fine.
- Save it (Ctrl+S) whenever you want. When you're done playing, tell me "done playing" and I'll read the whole
  file and turn every note into work.
- For a bug, it helps to say what you did, what happened and what you expected. F8 in the game saves a
  screenshot with a note at that moment.

Controls reminder
- Left mouse: cast. Once a fish bites: hold left mouse to reel. Mouse wheel: reel faster / slower.
- During a fight the mouse steers the rod (the view follows).
- E: grab the fish, put it in the cooler, open the cooler (also while carrying it), show it, take a fish out, sell.
- F: pick up / put down the cooler, close it, dump the fish out of a carried cooler you're showing, let a fish go.
- F6: the new debug menu (all key binds + mouse sensitivity).
- The prompt in the top-left always shows what E and F do right now.
- C: crouch. Z: prone. Shift: sprint. Space: jump.
