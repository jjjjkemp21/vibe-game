Controls reminder
- Left mouse: cast. Once a fish bites: hold left mouse to reel.
- During a fight the mouse steers the rod (the view follows). Mouse wheel: reel faster / slower.
- E: grab the fish, put it in the cooler, open the cooler, take a fish out, sell.
- F: pick up / put down the cooler, let a fish go / drop it.
- The prompt in the top-left always shows what E and F do right now.
- C: crouch. Z: prone. Shift: sprint. Space: jump.
