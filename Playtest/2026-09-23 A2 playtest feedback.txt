LURE - PLAYTEST FEEDBACK (Milestone A2)
=======================================

How to use this file
- Write anything, in any order, as short or long as you like. Bullet points are fine.
- Save it (Ctrl+S) whenever you want. When you're done playing, just tell me "done playing"
  and I'll read the whole file and turn every note into work.
- For a bug, it helps to say what you did, what happened and what you expected. You can also
  press F8 in the game to save a screenshot with a note at that moment.

Controls reminder
- Left mouse: cast. Once a fish bites: hold left mouse to reel.
- During a fight the mouse steers the rod (the view follows). Mouse wheel: reel faster / slower.
- E: grab the fish, put it in the cooler, take it out, pick up / put down the cooler, sell.
- F: let a fish go / drop it.
- C: crouch. Z: prone. Shift: sprint. Space: jump.
