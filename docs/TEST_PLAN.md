### Observations from T-008 testing (no failing test; for the lead)
- T008-O1: MaxModifiers keeps the first hits in lexical row-name order, so which traits survive the cap depends on their names (Albino and Feisty beat Heavy/Giant/QA_*). ### T-004 findings and notes (qa-engineer, 2026-09-22)
- T004-Q1 (minor, open, decision for the lead): after crawling off a ledge the character stays prone for up to 3 frames (50 ms) in the air. The stand-up is blocked while the wider stand capsule still overlaps the ledge corner; it stands up in the air once clear, never penetrates, and goes prone again on landing. `Stance.ProneWalksOffLedge` asserts at most 0.1 s. If "never prone in the air" must be strict, UnProne in the air needs another candidate position (e.g. away from the ledge).
- T004-Q2 (data pipeline, applies to EVERY DataTable): the engine's CSV import turns non-numeric text in a number cell (`fast`, `0.2x`) into 0 and reports NO import problem (`Fallback.NonNumericCellHandled` logs "import problems: 0"). For columns where 0 is valid, a typo becomes a silent 0. DT_Movement is guarded by `Data.NumericCellsAreNumbers`; every other `data/tables/DT_*.csv` (fish, rarity, modifier in T-008) needs the same raw-text check.
- T004-Q3 (info, gap): whether two IDENTICAL hand-built saved moves combine is not deterministic in a headless test world (walk/sprint observed as 0/0 in one run and 1/0 in the next; the moves come from AllocateNewMove outside the real client move pipeline). So bandwidth combining isn't asserted, only reported. `Net.MovesWithDifferentFlagsDoNotCombine` asserts the part that matters for correctness: moves with different sprint/prone/walk wishes never combine. If the playtester sees higher bandwidth or odd corrections while sprinting in 2-player PIE with net emulation, look here first.

Measure the "camera doesn't move" baseline only after the eye blend has finished (about 40 frames). ### T-032 open bugs (failing tests; each test is the regression test for its bug; owner unreal-engineer)

Measured cause: the rest length follows its target in float and stalls 5 float steps above the chord (rest - chord = 0.000610 cm at 1500 cm); the solver turns any excess into sag (+1 ulp = 0.30 cm, +5 ulp = 0.68 cm).
